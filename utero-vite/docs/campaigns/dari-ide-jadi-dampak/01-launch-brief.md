# Dari Ide Jadi Dampak - Campaign Launch Brief

## Campaign Decision

**Umbrella idea:** Dari Ide Jadi Dampak  
**Supporting line:** Punya ide? Wujudkan bersama.  
**Primary CTA:** Konsultasi Arah Brand  
**Offer:** Konsultasi awal gratis. Calon klien cukup membawa konteks bisnis, kebutuhan utama, dan target waktu.

Campaign keeps Utero's blunt "ideas need execution" character but uses collaborative acquisition language. It positions Utero as one integrated creative partner, not a list of disconnected services.

## Objective

Generate qualified B2B conversations that progress from WhatsApp inquiry to consultation, proposal, and won project.

Primary audience:

- Owners/founders planning growth, launch, expansion, or repositioning.
- Marketing/brand managers coordinating multiple creative needs.
- Operations/expansion teams opening outlets or rolling out signage.
- Initial geography: Malang Raya and East Java. Remote-friendly services can expand nationally after economics are proven.

## Message Architecture

1. **Direction before production** - Start with business goal and audience, not an asset list.
2. **One integrated team** - Strategy, design, activation, media, and technology move in one direction.
3. **Execution is the product** - Utero takes ideas beyond presentation into production and launch.
4. **Local fluency, long experience** - Since 1998, rooted in Malang, familiar with East Java business context.
5. **Partner, not vendor** - Collaboration continues through review and next-step decisions.

## Proof Allowed

- Utero states it has operated since 1998.
- Site states 25+ years of experience.
- Site presents seven integrated divisions.
- Site presents a structured identify-strategy-concept-activation-impact workflow.
- Site displays client and portfolio names, but campaign assets must not imply business outcomes without documented case evidence.

Avoid: best/number-one claims, guaranteed sales, guaranteed ROI, viral promises, unsupported project-result numbers.

## Funnel

1. Paid/organic content introduces execution problem.
2. Campaign landing page explains integrated approach.
3. WhatsApp CTA starts qualified conversation.
4. Sales captures company, role, city, need, timeline, and budget range.
5. Qualified lead books consultation.
6. Proposal follows complete brief and feasibility check.
7. CRM records won/lost outcome and reason.

A WhatsApp click is **contact intent**, not a lead. A lead exists only after a valid inquiry is received.

## Six-Week Launch

| Week | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Readiness | Validate routing, claims, service capacity, two usable project stories, tracking QA |
| 2 | Controlled launch | Publish landing page, organic launch set, Search and Meta learning campaigns |
| 3 | Lead quality | Review first 10 inquiries, search terms, routing, objections, and response time |
| 4 | Offer refinement | Promote strongest service/use-case angle; test branch-opening angle |
| 5 | Proof and retargeting | Publish verified case/process content; retarget engaged visitors |
| 6 | Scale decision | Reconcile spend, qualified pipeline, proposals, sales capacity, and margin |

## High-Intent Ad Angle

**Cabang Baru, Satu Pintu**: opening or refreshing an outlet requires brand direction, signage, promotional materials, and launch activity. Use as a service-specific ad set under umbrella campaign, not as company-wide positioning.

## Approval Gates

- One campaign WhatsApp route has named owner and response SLA.
- Landing and CTA events pass QA.
- UTM values persist into lead record or a documented manual process.
- Campaign has at least two verified, context-rich proof assets.
- Production and sales confirm capacity before media scaling.

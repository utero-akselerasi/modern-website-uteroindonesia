# Channel Plan

## Paid Media Split

Percentages apply to paid media only. Creative, CRM, tracking, and staff costs are separate.

| Use | Share | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Google Search nonbrand | 45% | Capture explicit service demand |
| Google Search brand | 5% | Protect and measure existing demand |
| Meta prospecting | 35% | Generate problem and offer awareness |
| Meta retargeting / click-to-WhatsApp | 10% | Re-engage qualified visitors |
| Controlled experiment reserve | 5% | Test one audience, offer, or format |
| LinkedIn organic | 0% media | Founder expertise and case credibility |
| WhatsApp nurture | operating cost | Qualification and sales follow-up |

## Google Search

Start exact and phrase match. Separate campaigns by service intent.

- Brand strategy: `konsultan branding malang`, `branding agency surabaya`, `jasa rebranding perusahaan`, `jasa brand identity`.
- Signage: `jasa signage surabaya`, `vendor neon box malang`, `pembuatan papan nama perusahaan`, `branding kendaraan perusahaan`.
- Digital: `digital marketing agency surabaya`, `agency social media malang`, `jasa kelola iklan perusahaan`.
- Branch opening angle: `vendor branding outlet`, `signage cabang baru`, `branding toko malang`.

Initial negatives: `lowongan`, `loker`, `gaji`, `magang`, `karir`, `tutorial`, `template`, `download`, `contoh proposal`.

## Meta

Prospecting creative:

1. Founder video: idea stuck between teams/vendors.
2. Carousel: strategy, visual, signage, launch, measurement.
3. Static: "Belum punya brief? Mulai dari bisnis, kebutuhan, target waktu."
4. Branch-opening carousel: one brief, one direction, coordinated rollout.

Retargeting creative:

- Process walkthrough.
- Relevant verified project story.
- Objection response: no brief, budget uncertainty, existing designer, timing.
- Direct consultation CTA.

Exclude active opportunities, recent qualified leads, existing customers where acquisition is objective, and opted-out contacts.

## LinkedIn Organic

Two posts per week from founder/leadership plus company-page amplification:

- Common execution gaps.
- How to brief a multi-channel creative project.
- When one integrated team is better than separate vendors.
- Verified project context: problem, scope, Utero role, visible result.

Measure qualified conversations and influenced opportunities, not impressions alone.

## WhatsApp Operating Script

Opening:

> Halo [nama], terima kasih sudah menghubungi Utero Indonesia melalui kampanye Dari Ide Jadi Dampak. Boleh ceritakan nama bisnis, kebutuhan utama, dan target waktunya?

Qualification:

> Siapa audiens utama dan tujuan komunikasinya? Apakah sudah ada materi acuan dan kisaran anggaran yang ingin dipertimbangkan?

Summary:

> Kami rangkum: [brand] membutuhkan [scope] untuk [audience/goal], target [date]. Setelah detail lengkap, tim mengecek ruang lingkup, jadwal, dan langkah berikutnya.

Follow-up: H+2 and H+5 only. Stop when prospect responds, declines, or opts out.

## Optimization Cadence

- Daily launch checks: spend, broken destinations, routing, sales coverage.
- Twice weekly: search terms, negatives, city mix, lead relevance.
- Weekly: CRM reconciliation, CPQL, consultation, proposal, response time.
- Fortnightly: one creative or offer test.
- Monthly: reallocations based on mature lead quality and contribution economics.
